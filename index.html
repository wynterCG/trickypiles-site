How to play

Three steps, endless levels

  1. 333555

    Each colour has a pile

    Every pile wants the next number of its own colour.

  2. 444444

    Tap the card that fits

    Play it from your hand onto its pile. Fast plays and combos score more.

  3. 666222

    Nothing fits? New hand

    Ask for a new hand and keep going until every pile is complete.

New rules every chapter

One new twist at a time

Each rule comes with a short tip the first time you meet it, and when the rules change, the game shows you what is new before you play.

  • Order

    When several cards fit, the smallest goes first.

  • 555

    Stars

    A star that fits must be played before anything else.

  • 777

    Moons

    A moon has to wait for every other card.

  • Counting down

    Some levels run backwards, from the top number to 1.

  • 78

    Double cards

    One card covers two numbers in a row.

  • 9

    Frozen piles

    A frozen pile waits until its key pile shows the number on the ice.

  • 9

    Locks

    A locked card opens once you play its key colour in the same hand.

  • 32

    Odd piles

    One pile counts in twos: 1, 3, 5 and so on.

  • Bosses

    Boss levels: only two lives, a faster clock or a surprise rule.

Every day

A new daily level, and boards to climb

Every day brings a new daily level with its own mix of rules, shown before you start. Keep your streak going and see how you rank.

  • Score points for every card, with bonuses for speed and combos.
  • Earn up to three stars by how high you score, and chase your best on every level.
  • Optional Google sign-in keeps your progress and puts you on the leaderboards.
